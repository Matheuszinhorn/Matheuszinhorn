## Respostas prontas
**Which app did you redesign, and which features did you pick?**
> Jupiter (jup.ag). I redesigned three features of the Swap flow for first-time users: (1) the swap form, with plain-language order types and the quote, fees and minimum received shown before connecting; (2) a new review step before signing, with a guaranteed minimum, a price-impact meter and a note on what the wallet will ask; (3) the connect-wallet drawer, which explains what a wallet is, shows detected wallets, gives people without a wallet a path forward and warns about recovery-phrase phishing. Desktop and mobile for each.

**What was the biggest problem you found in the current design, in 2–3 sentences?**
> A newcomer sees "Connect" as the main action before seeing any price, fee or minimum received, so they are asked to commit before they can decide. Key terms (Market/Limit/DCA, Ultra, Paste CA, the V1 transactions toggle, slippage) appear without explanation, and risk numbers are bare percentages with no sense of whether they are good or bad.
