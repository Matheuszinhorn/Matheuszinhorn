## Descrição
Your website looks fine on a computer, but on a phone it opens tiny and visitors have to pinch and zoom to read your phone number. Most visitors give up.

I fix that **without redesigning your site**: same content, same colors, same logo. I add the missing mobile settings and a small set of CSS rules that only apply on phones. Your desktop version stays exactly as it is.

**What you get**
- Readable text and a usable menu on phones
- No horizontal scrolling
- Tap-to-call and directions buttons (Standard/Premium)
- Before/after screenshots at phone and desktop sizes
- The changed files, or the changes applied, if you give temporary access

**Works with:** hand-coded HTML/CSS sites and most older templates. For WordPress, Wix or Squarespace, message me first so I can confirm what is possible.

**How I work:** I use AI-assisted tools to speed up the analysis and the CSS, and I review and test every change on real phone and desktop sizes before delivery. I never deliver the same work to two clients.
